## 1. Engagement Framing (SCQ Framework)

### Situation
Kestrel Bank is a mid-sized Indian private-sector commercial bank handling approximately **40,000 retail complaints per month** across four operational business units: Credit Cards, Savings & Current Accounts (CASA), Digital Banking, and Branch Escalations. Complaint operations are staffed by ~120 frontline agents.

### Complication
Complaint inflow is growing faster than headcount due to rapid digital channel adoption (72% web/app intake). Agents spend up to 15 minutes per case searching across four disconnected policy repositories, resulting in ticket misrouting, rework, and communication drift. 

Under the **Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 (RB-IOS 2026)**, customers may escalate to the RBI Ombudsman if a complaint remains unresolved after **30 calendar days** or is closed unsatisfactorily. In the public baseline data, ~12% of complaints drift past the 30-day threshold, and 3.0% escalate to the Ombudsman, exposing the bank to regulatory audits, operational penalties, and compensation awards up to **₹30 lakh per case**.

### Key Question
> **"Where should Kestrel Bank deploy Artificial Intelligence in complaint handling, what financial and operational returns will it generate, and how can the bank roll it out safely without regulatory, data privacy, or customer trust risks?"**

## 2. Hypothesis-Driven Issue Tree

```
Where should Kestrel Bank use AI in complaint handling, what will it return, and how do we roll it out safely?
│
├── 1. Where is operational value lost today? (Diagnostic & Discovery)
│   ├── 1.1 Which complaint issues and process stages drive volume and delay? [A1 Diagnostic]
│   └── 1.2 Which root causes require operational process/policy fixes without any AI? [A1 Six Fixes]
│
├── 2. Which AI use cases are worth deploying? (Prioritization & Evaluation)
│   ├── 2.1 What is the simplest, lowest-cost technology rung that solves each pain point? [Decision Ladder]
│   └── 2.2 How do shortlisted use cases score on a 6-criteria Value vs. Feasibility matrix? [Scorecard]
│
├── 3. What is the commercial and operational return? (Business Case & Sensitivity)
│   ├── 3.1 What is the true Total Cost of Ownership (build capex, run teams, token economics)? [A2 Model]
│   └── 3.2 What are the Hard vs. Soft financial benefits, and which assumptions create risk? [Tornado Chart]
│
└── 4. How do we execute safely and responsibly? (Governance & Roadmap)
    ├── 4.1 How do DPDP Act 2025/2027, RBI FREE-AI Sutras, and RB-IOS 2026 constrain design? [Control Map]
    ├── 4.2 Where must Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) authority be legally enforced? [HITL Rules]
    └── 4.3 How do we structure a phased, stage-gated implementation roadmap with kill gates? [12-Month Plan]
```
