## 1. Stakeholder Matrix (Influence vs. Interest)

| Stakeholder Persona | Role in the Initiative | Influence | Interest | Core Priorities & Concerns | Engagement & Alignment Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Head of Customer Service** | **Executive Sponsor**; owns operational budget and staffing | **High** | **High** | Reducing escalation rates, customer CSAT, handling cost per case, capacity headroom. | Bi-weekly steering committee meetings; owns business case approval. |
| **Principal Nodal Officer (PNO)**| **Statutory Redress Owner**; accountable to RBI for compliance | **High** | **High** | 30-day RB-IOS SLA compliance, Ombudsman inquiry volume, quality of formal replies. | Dedicated interviews; co-signs the AI Governance & Control Map. |
| **Chief Risk / Compliance Officer (CRO)** | **Gatekeeper / Risk Sign-off**; can pause or veto rollout | **High** | **Medium**| DPDP personal data compliance, model hallucinations, bias, customer disclosure under FREE-AI. | Early design reviews; formal sign-off at each stage gate. |
| **Chief Information Officer (CIO) / IT Architecture** | **Delivery Partner**; provisions hosting, CRM integration, security | **High** | **Medium**| Zero core-banking modifications in Year 1, API latency (<5s), data residency, vendor lock-in. | Joint architecture workshops; sign-off on Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs). |
| **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)** | **Capital Allocator**; approves capex & software spend | **High** | **Low** | Clear payback within 24 months, hard labor cost realization, transparent token pricing risk. | Stage-gated funding proposal; separate Hard vs. Full benefit presentations. |
| **Complaint Team Leads** | **Operational Supervisors**; lead 4 frontline units | **Medium**| **High** | Shift queue workload, QA review burden, edge-case escalation paths, agent training. | Operational interviews; pilot site design; weekly feedback reviews. |
| **Frontline Complaint Agents** | **Daily End Users** (~120 agents across 4 teams) | **Low** | **High** | Less manual searching, fewer draft rewrites, reassurance they are not being replaced by AI. | Contextual inquiry sessions; identify frontline "AI Champions"; change management. |
