# Flagship A: 30-Minute Operational Interview Guide
**Client**: Kestrel Bank | **Module**: 04_FlagshipA/discovery/02_interview_guide.md | **Target**: Operations, Compliance, Frontline Leads  

---

## 1. Interview Methodology & Ethical Guidelines
* **Target Audience**: 3 to 5 operational professionals in bank operations, customer care, and grievance redress.
* **Duration**: Exactly 30 minutes.
* **Confidentiality Rule**: Never solicit proprietary customer or confidential bank records. Use interview responses to validate structural hypotheses and calibrate assumptions (`AS-01` through `AS-16`).

---

## 2. 5-Section Interview Script

### Section 1: Role & Workflow Warm-Up (3 Minutes)
* *"What is your specific role, and which team/product do you support (Cards, CASA, Digital, Branch)?"*
* *"How does a complaint physically reach your queue? Is it automatically pushed or manually claimed?"*

### Section 2: End-to-End Case Walkthrough (10 Minutes)
* *"Think back to the last complex complaint you personally handled from start to finish. Walk me through every step."*
* *"Where in that journey did the ticket sit idle waiting for an input, approval, or customer response?"*
* *"When you had to research policy or circular guidelines, where did you search? How many separate applications or document stores did you open?"*
* *"How many different individuals or specialist teams touched the case before resolution?"*

### Section 3: Operating Metrics & Baseline Grounding (7 Minutes)
* *"Roughly how many minutes does an agent spend actively investigating and drafting a response for an average case?"* (Validates `AS-02`: 25 min AHT).
* *"In your experience, what percentage of complaints risk drifting beyond the 30-day statutory threshold?"* (Validates 12% drift assumption).
* *"Which 2 or 3 complaint types consume the most emotional energy and administrative time?"*

### Section 4: Friction Points & AI Boundaries (7 Minutes)
* *"If you could eliminate one frustrating, non-value-add task from your day tomorrow, what would it be?"*
* *"If an AI tool could instantly retrieve and cite the exact governing RBI circular clause for a disputed fee, would you trust it?"*
* *"Where would you definitely NOT trust an AI suggestion or automated response? Where MUST a human review and sign off?"*

### Section 5: Closing & Referrals (3 Minutes)
* *"What critical friction point or constraint did I fail to ask about that I really should know?"*
* *"Who else on your team or in compliance handles difficult edge cases and would have a strong perspective?"*
